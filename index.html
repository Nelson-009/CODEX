About CODEX

Understand the code. Understand the machine.

CODEX is an evolving encyclopedia for programming knowledge.


Its goal is simple: explain technical ideas clearly enough that you can reason about them—not simply copy them.


Concepts are structured around what they are, why they matter, how they work, examples, common mistakes and what happens under the hood.

Built for curiosity.

From your first variable to the architecture behind serious applications, CODEX is designed to grow with the learner.


Created and developed by Nelson Atelier.
